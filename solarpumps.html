SOLAR WATER PUMP

Solar water pumps can supply water to locations which are beyond the reach of power lines. Commonly, such places relay on
human or animal power or on diesel engines for their water supply Solar water pumps can replace the current pump systems
and result in both social and economic benefits as well as climate related benefits. The water supplied by the solar water pump
can be used to irrigate crops, water livestock or provide potable drinking water.

A solar water pump system is essentially an electrical pump system in which the electricity is provided by one or several
PhotoVoltaic (PV) panels. A typical solar powered pumping system consists of a solar panel array that powers an electric motor,
which in turn powers a bore or surface pump. The water is often pumped from the ground or stream into a storage tank that
provides a gravity feed, so energy storage is not needed for these systems.
